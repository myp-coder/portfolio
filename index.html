About

Where I come from shapes how I sell.

I grew up in Saudi Arabia, studied in Turkey, and now work in Dubai. Moving between these places taught me how business actually gets done in the GCC and in Turkey, and I switch between Turkish, English and Arabic depending on who's in the room.

At CEQUENS, I help companies across the GCC reach their customers on the channels people actually use: WhatsApp, SMS and voice. Before that, at Odoo, I owned the Turkish market's partner channel from the first cold call to a live, revenue generating partnership: 2,000+ calls, 400+ meetings, and 60+ new candidates qualified every month. Earlier, I built and ran Lume Studio, an AI content studio with clients across Germany, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait and the UAE, closing $15,000+ in new business through cold outreach alone with no existing brand behind me. I still use AI daily, especially Claude, to research faster and cover more ground than most people can.
